# Image credits

Every real photograph used by the demo (backend catalogue images, site and admin banners) is listed here.

All photos are free for commercial use **without any attribution obligation**; credit is given voluntarily.

* **Wikimedia Commons** files were checked through the Commons API (`extmetadata.LicenseShortName`); only files whose licence is `CC0` or `Public domain` were kept.
* **Pexels** photos were checked on their source page (labelled "Free" and linked to the Pexels License, https://www.pexels.com/license/ - free for commercial use, no attribution required, modification allowed).
* No photo shows a legible real brand mark: four marks that were visible (a battery label, a device sticker, a glove emboss and the tape-reel lettering) were removed with Pillow/OpenCV inpainting; images are otherwise only cropped, resized and re-encoded.
* Brand logos (`brands/*.png`), `logo.png` and `payments/*.png` are synthetic (generated by `scripts/demo/generate-images.py`).
